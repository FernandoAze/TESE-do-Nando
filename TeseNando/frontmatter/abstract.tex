\chapter*{Abstract}

\ac{MIR} visualization tools play a crucial role in music analysis, annotation, and interactive music applications. Accurate visual representation of audio and algorithmic outputs is essential for the evaluation, analysis, and annotation of \ac{MIR} tasks. This thesis develops LayerIt, a Python‑based object-oriented (\ac{OO}) tool that combines established \ac{MIR} resources. The developed project emphasize modularity and reusability, the design defines clear object boundaries for data sources, score aligned visualization layers, simplifying the addition of new data types and comparison modes. Visuals are exported as \ac{SVG} to allow further customization and future integration with web or interactive tools. As a validation case, we use LayerIt to visualize and compare beat tracking outputs, demonstrating how score aligned visualizations support analysis and evaluation of annotated and algorithmic \ac{MIR} data. The project is presented as a foundation for further development toward a mature framework for specialized \ac{MIR} visualizations.
